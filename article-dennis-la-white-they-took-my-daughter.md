## Title

Dennis L.A. White Goes Dark as Curtis in Starz Thriller "They Took My Daughter"

## Slug

dennis-la-white-they-took-my-daughter-starz

## Dek

The actor, producer, and director trades legacy-building for menace in Tim Cruz's directorial debut — now streaming on Starz.

## Body

Dennis L.A. White is stepping into unfamiliar territory. In "They Took My Daughter," the Starz thriller that premiered July 1, White plays Curtis — a departure from the legacy-building roles that have defined much of his recent work, and a reminder that range is exactly what separates a working actor from a star.

The film centers on a mother's desperate, undercover mission to rescue her college-age daughter after she vanishes from a seductive VIP nightclub, unraveling a web of corruption along the way. It marks the feature directorial debut of Tim Cruz, and stars Vanessa Simmons and Darrin Dewitt Henson alongside White, Angelique Valentine, Jordi Vilasuso, Angela Simmons, Angelina Folino, Denicia Hamilton Chance, and Victoria Gabrielle Platt.

The project was executive produced by Tressa Azarel Smallwood, founder of MegaMind Media, alongside film financier Donte Lee — part of a growing wave of Black-led thrillers finding a home on Starz's platform.

For White, known equally for his work in front of and behind the camera, Curtis is a chance to show a colder register than audiences are used to seeing from him — proof that the same instincts that make him a compelling producer also make him a dangerous scene partner.

"They Took My Daughter" is streaming now on Starz.
